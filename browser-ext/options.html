MoorAI Browser Guard

On-device, content-free prompt review for ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot.

Content-free by design: the text you type is scanned locally in your browser. Only a category, a risk level, and a one-way hash are ever sent — never your prompt.

Mode
MoorAI console (optional)

Where content-free signals are reported. Uses the same POST /api/alerts contract and X-Install-Token header as the MoorAI agent. Leave blank to run purely locally (no reporting).
